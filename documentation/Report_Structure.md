# Power BI Report Structure

## Page 1: Overview

**KPI Cards (top row):**
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin %
- Total Orders
- Average Order Value

**Visuals:**
- Line chart: Sales by Month (based on OrderDate)
- Bar chart or Map: Sales by Region
- Donut chart: Sales by Category
- Table: Top 5 Products by Sales Amount

**Filters / Slicers:**
- Region
- Category
- Date range

---

## Page 2: Product Analysis

- Clustered bar chart: Sales & Profit by Category
- Treemap: Product performance
- Matrix: Category × Region with Sales values
- Scatter chart: Quantity vs SalesAmount (bubble size = Profit)

---

## Page 3: Customer Insights

- Bar chart: Sales by Customer
- Card: Unique Customers count
- Table with conditional formatting: Customer details (Sales, Profit, Orders)

---

## Page 4: Trends & Comparison (optional)

- Area chart for monthly sales trends
- Year-over-Year comparison

---

## Design Guidelines

- Primary color: #0078D4 (Microsoft blue)
- Positive values: Green
- Negative values: Red
- Font: Segoe UI

I kept the structure simple and easy to read. You can add extra slicers or bookmarks if needed.
